About the Portal

Centralized Digital Lost & Found Portal

Our Mission

Every semester, hundreds of valuable student belongings—laptops, identification badges, textbooks, electronic accessories, and keys—go missing across classrooms, libraries, laboratories, and dining halls. Before CampusConnect, students were forced to rely on scattered social media posts, crowded bulletin boards, or repeatedly checking with various departmental security desks.

CampusConnect was engineered to bridge this gap. We provide a single, verified, university-wide platform where lost property can be reported in seconds, discovered belongings can be published safely, and verifiable claim workflows ensure items are reunited with their genuine owners.

Role-Based Security

Integrated with institutional student and staff roles. Faculty and campus security personnel have access to dedicated moderation and verification tools.

Verified Ownership

Claim requests mandate distinctive verification details (such as serial numbers or private markings), safeguarding against fraudulent claims.

Campus Security Partnership

CampusConnect operates in full coordination with the Campus Security Department. Any unclaimed high-value items submitted to departmental offices are securely logged and transferred to the Main Security Control Center.
