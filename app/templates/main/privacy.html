CampusConnect Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Academic Term 2026

1. Information We Collect

CampusConnect collects essential institutional profile details during registration, including your full name, official college email address, student or employee identification number, departmental affiliation, and telephone number. When submitting property reports, description texts, campus locations, timestamps, and optional photographs are recorded.

2. How Personal Data Is Protected

Your personal telephone number and student identification roll number are never displayed publicly to anonymous visitors. Contact between users is facilitated through authenticated in-app notifications and regulated claim submissions. Sensitive verification answers are accessible only to the listing owner and authorized campus security moderators.

3. File Upload Integrity & Media Storage

Uploaded images undergo strict file header inspection and are assigned randomized cryptographic identifiers before storage. Files are kept in isolated storage directories and scrubbed of executable scripts.

4. Audit Logs and Accountability

To maintain safety, all logins, administrative status updates, claim reviews, and listing removals are logged with timestamps and originating IP addresses for audit trail compliance.

5. Contact Campus Data Protection

For privacy inquiries or account deactivation requests, please contact privacy@campusconnect.edu or visit the Campus IT Information Security Division.
