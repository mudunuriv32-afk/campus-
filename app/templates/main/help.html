Quick User Guides

Q: What should I do immediately after losing an item?

1. Sign in to your account and click Report Lost Item.
2. Enter the exact campus building, floor, room number, or outdoor landmark where you last had the item.
3. Check the Found Items page to see if someone has already surrendered it.

Q: I picked up a lost item. Where do I store it?

Post a quick listing using Report Found Item so the owner knows it is safe. If the item is high-value (laptop, wallet, keys, phone), please hand it over to the nearest faculty lab in-charge or take it directly to the Central Security Control Room.

Q: How do I prove an item belongs to me?

Click "Request Claim" on the item page and specify identifiable marks that are not visible in public photos (e.g. laptop MAC address or serial number, lock-screen wallpaper, stickers on the bottom, or exact cash denominations in a wallet).
