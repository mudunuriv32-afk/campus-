Terms of Service & Campus Policy

Effective: Academic Year 2026

1. Acceptable Campus Use

CampusConnect is strictly designated for registered college students, faculty, and authorized campus staff. Users agree to submit accurate, truthful, and lawful descriptions of lost or found belongings. Submitting fraudulent reports, spam, abusive language, or unauthorized promotional material is prohibited and subject to institutional disciplinary action.

2. False Claims & Theft Policy

Submitting false claim requests on items that do not belong to you constitutes theft of property under institutional codes of conduct and local legal statutes. All claim requests are permanently tied to verified student IDs and logged in audit trails.

3. Property Handover Safety

Users are advised to conduct in-person property handovers during daylight hours or at designated campus locations such as the Campus Security Control Center, Library Information Desk, or Academic Department offices.

4. Unclaimed Property Retention

Found property logged on CampusConnect and surrendered to campus custody will be retained in accordance with university policy (90 calendar days) before being donated or disposed of per institutional guidelines.

5. Disclaimer of Liability

While CampusConnect provides the technological infrastructure to connect campus community members, the university cannot guarantee the condition or recovery of all lost items.
