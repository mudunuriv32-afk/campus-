How CampusConnect Works

1. Publish Report

Submit a lost item description or post a found item. Attach photos, specific campus landmarks, and dates. Private identifying details are safeguarded.

2. Smart Matching & Claims

Our campus algorithm matches lost listings against found property. If you recognize your item, submit a verified claim with proof of ownership.

3. Verified Handover

The finder reviews the claim. Once verified and approved, coordinate return via in-app instructions or meet at the Campus Security Control Center.

Platform Highlights

Engineered for Campus Trust & Privacy

CampusConnect eliminates scattered social media lost-and-found posts and paper flyers with a centralized, verified campus portal.

Private Verification Details

Finders never see phone numbers or private identifying details publicly. Claimants must provide secret verifiable proof.

Automated Matching Engine

The system automatically cross-references dates, categories, and campus landmarks to alert users of potential matches.

Staff & Security Integration

Campus security and lab staff can act as official custodians, logging surrendered property into a secure audit trail.
